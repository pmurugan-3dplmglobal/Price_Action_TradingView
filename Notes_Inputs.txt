Scan Export Spot Trend & Target Annotator (Separate Utility)

  • Concept: A non-intrusive background/CLI helper script (e.g., annotate_scan_export_with_spot.py) that enriches the daily CSV export without affecting the core trading engines.
  • How it works:
      1. export from scan tap to & Reads the exported CSV file from output/exports/.
      2. For each staged trade row, extracts the underlying stock symbol (e.g., BAJAJFINSV from BAJAJFINSV26AUG1920PE).
      3. Fetches the underlying spot candles and evaluates:
          • New Column A (Spot_Trend): BULL or BEAR (based on spot candle structure & ABC pattern).
          • New Column B (Spot_T1_Target): The calculated spot T1 target derived from Negation Theory.
      4. Saves an enriched version of the CSV (e.g., scan_export_enriched_DD_MM_YY.csv).
